# Engineering Intent

Forge aims to make governed AI engineering predictable, maintainable, secure, and economically responsible.

This document is informative. Normative requirements are maintained in the [Engineering Constitution](standards/engineering-constitution.md) and [Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Desired outcomes

- trustworthy and reviewable decisions;
- clear capability ownership;
- reproducible execution evidence;
- minimal data disclosure;
- understandable operational behavior;
- replaceable external integrations;
- incremental delivery without premature platform complexity.

## Trade-off order

When concerns conflict, evaluate safety, correctness, simplicity, maintainability, observability, performance, cost, and convenience—in that order unless an accepted decision documents a justified exception.

Success is measured through approved contracts and repeatable evidence, not through the number of components or technologies adopted.
