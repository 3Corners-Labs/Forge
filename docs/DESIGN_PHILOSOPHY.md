# Design Philosophy

This document explains intent and is not normative. The [Engineering Constitution](standards/engineering-constitution.md), accepted ADRs, and [Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md) take precedence.

Forge favors:

- capabilities over products;
- explicit contracts over implicit coupling;
- modularity over premature distribution;
- policy before consequential execution;
- minimal, attributable context over ad-hoc prompts;
- evidence over architectural claims;
- reversible evolution over speculative complexity;
- human accountability over autonomous authority.

Technology choices remain adapters unless an accepted ADR says otherwise.
